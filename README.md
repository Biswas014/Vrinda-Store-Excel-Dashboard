# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard (Excel Project)

🎓 **My first Excel data analysis project as part of my data analyst learning journey.**

This project explores Vrinda Store's sales data to identify key customer insights, top-performing channels, and regional sales contributions through an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 🧠 Project Objective
To analyze Vrinda Store's sales data and uncover actionable insights on:
- 📅 Monthly sales and order trends  
- 👩 Gender-based sales performance  
- 🛍️ Channel contribution  
- 🗺️ State-wise total sales  
- 👥 Age group and gender-based order proportions  

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Process

1️⃣ **Data Import & Understanding**  
   • Imported the raw Excel dataset into **Power Query**.  
   • Checked column types, missing values, and structure.

2️⃣ **Data Cleaning & Transformation**  
   • Removed duplicates and unnecessary columns.  
   • Handled null values and standardized data formats.  
   • Loaded the cleaned output into Excel as a **Table**.

3️⃣ **Data Analysis (Pivot Tables)**  
   • Created Pivot Tables to answer key business questions:  
     - Total orders vs total sales (Month-wise)  
     - Sales distribution by Gender  
     - Order status proportions (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, etc.)  
     - State-wise total sales  
     - Channel-wise order contribution  
     - Age group and gender-wise order proportion

4️⃣ **Dashboard Design**  
   • Built an interactive dashboard with Pivot Charts.  
   • Added **two slicers (Month & Category)** for easy filtering.  
   • Applied consistent formatting and labels for clarity.

5️⃣ **Insights & Recommendations**  
   • Derived findings from the visualized charts to support data-driven decisions.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
1️⃣ Women customers are more likely to buy than men (~65%).  
2️⃣ Amazon, Myntra & Flipkart together contribute ~80% of total orders.  
3️⃣ The 30–49 years age group contributes ~50% of the total orders.  
4️⃣ Maharashtra, Karnataka & Uttar Pradesh together account for ~35% of total sales.

---

## 🎯 Final Recommendation
**Target women customers aged 30–49 years living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh by promoting ads, offers, and coupons on Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart.**  
This focused marketing strategy can improve engagement and maximize ROI on promotional campaigns.
